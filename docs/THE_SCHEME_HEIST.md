# The Scheme Heist

In **Scheme** we plot, we **Racket** and run—  
**Larceny** nicks the heap; **Grift**'s the con.  
**Guile** in the REPL, **Gambit** on the stack,  
**Steel** in the bank where we never give back.

**Chez** the **Chicken**, **Chibi** and **Kawa** brew;  
**Gauche** to the left, **Bigloo** in the queue.  
**Clojure** in the JVM, **Arc** in the sky—  
one **Lisp** to bind them, one paren to tie.

So here's to the dialects, crooked and clean:  
we **Scheme** in the open and run on the **Steel**.  
**Grift** in the arena, **Racket** in the shell—  
the only **Larceny** that never goes to jail.
